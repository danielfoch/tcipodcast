The Canadian Real Estate Investor · Episode 441

Why Are Data Centres So Controversial?

Published 25 Sep 2026 · 45:05

Listen to this episode

Episode notes

Mississauga and Oakville just froze new data centres. Saskatchewan rolled out the red carpet for a Bell campus near Regina worth over $50 billion. Nick and Daniel break down what these buildings are, why each province is handling them so differently, and why two out of three Canadians don't want one near their home. They take on the viral water claims and explain how the money works: tenants reserve power and pay whether they use it or not. Then they each say whether they'd invest. Plus the October 27 date that could decide whether the GTA fringe becomes a market or stays frozen.
