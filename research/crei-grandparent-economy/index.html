Slide 20 · Block 10 · How estates are taxed

Hypothetical cottage: a $700,000 gain puts $350,000 on the final tax return

Cottage held until death Hypothetical example

$18,750

Our math Ontario probate on a $1.3M house, at $15 per $1,000 over $50,000

Death is a deemed sale at fair market value. A principal residence is exempt. A spouse gets a rollover, so the tax waits for the second death.

A cottage, rental or second property has a capital gain with 50% included. The increase to two-thirds was cancelled on March 21, 2025.

Alberta caps probate at $525. B.C. charges 1.4% over $50,000.

Source: Canada Revenue Agency, capital gains at death. Prime Minister’s Office, Mar 21, 2025 (inclusion rate). TaxTips.ca, Ontario probate fees, Nov 20, 2026. The cottage is a made-up example from the run sheet.

Not tax or legal advice. Talk to an estate lawyer and an accountant. No data on cottage succession or capital gains exposure was verified.
