Power generation backlog: data and sources

As of 26 November 2026. Native USD/EUR billions; no currency conversion. Machine-readable data

Methodology and limits

  • Quarter-end stocks of outstanding equipment and service commitments; neither quarterly order intake nor company-wide RPO.
  • Calendar quarter-end dates. Siemens fiscal Q4 ends September 30 and is calendar Q3.
  • Indexed chart: each native-currency value divided by its 2022-12-31 value, multiplied by 100. No FX conversion or USD/EUR equivalence is implied.
  • GE Vernova 2022–2024 uses historical reported Power figures. 2025–2026 uses the 2026 reporting structure and recast 2025 comparatives. The dotted connection at 2025 marks this basis change; this is not a constant-scope growth series.
  • GE Vernova sold a portion of Steam Power nuclear activities to EDF in Q2 2024, reducing Power RPO by approximately USD 3.9bn. This affects the 2024 dip.
  • In 2026, Steam Power was reassigned among Gas, Nuclear and Hydro, and part of Electrification Software moved into Gas Power. See GE Q3 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 1 and MD&A.
  • GE Vernova Power includes gas, nuclear and hydro; Siemens Gas Services includes equipment and servicing. Neither series measures exclusively gas turbines or AI demand. Contracted backlog is not delivered generating capacity.
  • No verified GE Power observations before 2022-12-31 or Siemens Gas Services observations before 2022-09-30 are plotted. Missing values remain null; no estimates.
  • All 16 GE observations were independently checked against the linked original reports. All 17 Siemens observations were freshly retrieved from Fiscal.ai; the September 2026 endpoint was also checked against the original Siemens earnings release. Earlier Siemens quarters were not each separately checked against original reports.
