Ellen DiMauro, Semafor, Nov. 26, 2026. Banks look to dump AI debt. Photo: Daniel Cole/Reuters, via Semafor. People familiar with the pitches. No deal size in the piece.

Where does the SRT risk end up: private credit, insurers, pensions? Some of this is December balance-sheet cleanup. Some banks say they are making room to lend more.
